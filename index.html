§ 02 — Directions

My work sits between understanding models and shipping them — probing how neural networks represent information, and turning that understanding into systems that hold up in industrial settings.

R/01
Neural Network Interpretability
Probing ANN internals — what individual neurons and layers respond to, and how activations organise inside a trained network.
activation analysis · feature attribution
R/02
Model Analysis
Empirical study of network behaviour on benchmark vision datasets such as CIFAR-10 — how architecture and training choices shape learned representations.
benchmarks · representation study
R/03
Neural Architectures
Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks and learnable spline activations as alternatives to fixed-activation MLPs, evaluated on real scientific data.
KAN · ChebyKAN · FasterKAN
R/04
Symbolic Regression
Recovering closed-form, interpretable expressions from trained models — bridging black-box accuracy and human-readable science.
auto_symbolic · interpretable ML
R/05
Efficient ML
Pruning and compression experiments — how much of a network can be removed before accuracy degrades, and what that reveals about redundancy.
pruning · compression
R/06
Computer Vision
Detection, segmentation and image analysis — from research prototypes to metrology-grade inspection pipelines.
detection · segmentation
R/07
Industrial ML
Deploying machine learning inside manufacturing and metrology — where robustness, calibration and workflow integration matter as much as accuracy.
deployment · robustness
R/08
LLM Systems
Retrieval-augmented generation and agentic pipelines that make quantitative models controllable through natural language.
RAG · agents · sktime
