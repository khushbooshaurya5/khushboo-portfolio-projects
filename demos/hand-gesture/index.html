Real-time Hand Gesture Recognition

Runs entirely in your browser. MediaPipe tracks 21 3D landmarks per hand at video frame-rate; a small geometric classifier reads finger states into gestures. No frames ever leave your device — the model and inference run client-side on the GPU via WebAssembly.
