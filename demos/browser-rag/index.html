Private RAG in Your Browser

A full RAG pipeline, 100% client-side. Documents are chunked, embedded with MiniLM, retrieved by cosine similarity, and answered by a local LLM (Qwen2.5-0.5B) that runs on your GPU via transformers.js / WebGPU — grounded in the retrieved text, with citations. No API key, no server, nothing uploaded.
