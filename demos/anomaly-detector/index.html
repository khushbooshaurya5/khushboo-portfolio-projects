Anomaly & Defect Detector

Real model, live in your browser. A convolutional autoencoder trained only on normal images (FashionMNIST "Trouser") learns to reconstruct what *normal* looks like — so a high reconstruction error localizes anomalies and defects it never saw in training. Unsupervised, no labels. Same idea as my industrial defect-detection work, running client-side via ONNX.
