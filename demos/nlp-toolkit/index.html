What each view does

  • Sentences — boundary detection that isn't just splitting on "." (handles e.g., Dr., decimals, abbreviations)
  • POS tags — every token labelled Noun / Verb / Adjective / … from context, not a dictionary lookup
  • Terms — multi-word noun phrases (#Adjective* #Noun+) ranked by frequency — the backbone of keyword & terminology extraction
  • Entities — people, places, organisations, dates and numeric values pulled out by tag
