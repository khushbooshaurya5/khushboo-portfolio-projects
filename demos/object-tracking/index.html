Real-time Multi-Object Tracking

Detection + tracking, live in your browser. A MediaPipe detector (EfficientDet-Lite) finds objects each frame; a from-scratch ByteTrack-style tracker then links them across frames into persistent tracks — each box keeps its ID and colour as it moves, with a motion trail. The tracker's two-stage association recovers objects through low-confidence frames (blur, partial occlusion) instead of dropping their ID. Everything runs client-side; no frame leaves your device.

What you're seeing

  • Coloured box + #ID — one persistent track; the ID survives motion & brief occlusion
  • Trail — the track's recent path (constant-velocity motion model)
  • Two-stage matching — high-confidence detections matched first, then leftover tracks recovered from the low-confidence tail (the ByteTrack idea)

Private by design: the camera stream stays in the page — nothing is uploaded or recorded.
