Neural Machine Translation

A translation model, running on your device. A MarianMT encoder-decoder (the Helsinki-NLP opus-mt family) runs entirely in your browser via transformers.js — no API, no server, nothing uploaded. The model (~30–75 MB, quantised) downloads once on first use and is cached; after that, translation is fully offline.

What's happening

  • Encoder-decoder — the source is encoded, then the target is generated token-by-token with beam search
  • opus-mt — one specialised model per language direction (no giant multilingual model needed)
  • ONNX + WASM — the model runs through onnxruntime-web; first load fetches & caches the weights, then it's local
  • This is the runtime side of the companion nmt-finetune-eval repo, which fine-tunes & scores these models (BLEU/COMET)

Private by design. Your text is translated on your device and never sent anywhere.
